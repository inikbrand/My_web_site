Privacy Policy

Who I am. This site belongs to Iryna Nikolaieva, a freelance marketer and graphic designer based in Cardiff, United Kingdom.

What I collect. Only what you type into the contact form: your name, phone number and email address. Nothing else is gathered — there are no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies used for profiling.

Why I collect it. Solely to reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project. Your details are never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Where it goes. Submissions are delivered to a private Google Form and stored in Google Drive under my account. Google acts as the data processor; their handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

How long I keep it. Enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. Records tied to a signed contract are kept for six years, as UK accounting rules require.

Your rights. Under UK GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Write to me through any of the contact links in the footer and I will action the request within one month.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can raise it with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
